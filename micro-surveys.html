About these micro-surveys

Each I-REACCH micro-survey has one question and takes less than a minute. Choose a micro-survey below, complete it in Microsoft Forms, then mark it completed in this browser.

They are open to all research staff, research-enabling staff and PGRs. You may be asked to log in with your University of Leicester email; the system won’t collect your name or email address. Contact the I-REACCH team if you would like more information before taking part.

By completing the survey, you confirm that you have read the information above and consent to take part in the study.
